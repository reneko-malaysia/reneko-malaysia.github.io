ABOUT

What We Do

Rescue, Rehome & Return — three values at the heart of everything we do for every single cat.

01

Rescue

We rescue stray and abandoned cats within our means. As a small team, we may not be able to take in cases needing intensive medical care, and encourage reaching out to larger organizations when needed.

02

Rehome

We find loving homes for rescued cats, so they can enjoy warm beds, full meals, and the comfort they deserve! Please refer to “Adoption Process” for more information.

03

Return

We carry out Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR), helping control the stray population humanely while improving the cats’ quality of life.

MEET THE DUO

Riko & Rina
